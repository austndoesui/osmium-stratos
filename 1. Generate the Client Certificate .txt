1. Generate the Client Certificate and Key
Run this command in your terminal (Linux, macOS, or Windows with OpenSSL):

openssl req -x509 -newkey rsa:4096 -keyout client.key -out client.crt -days 365 -nodes -subj "/CN=osmium-client"

This creates two files in your current directory:
client.crt (the client certificate)
client.key (the private key)


2. Add the Certificate to the LXD Server’s Trust Store
Copy client.crt to your LXD server (if you generated it elsewhere), then on the LXD server run:

lxc config trust add client.crt

This will add the certificate to the LXD server’s trust store and allow it to connect.

3. Copy the Certificate and Key Contents
On Linux/macOS:

cat client.crt
cat client.key

4. Add to the Osmium Panel
Go to your panel’s “Add Host” form.
Paste the contents of client.crt into the Client Certificate (PEM) field.
Paste the contents of client.key into the Client Key (PEM) field.
(Optional) If your LXD server uses a custom CA, paste its certificate into the CA Certificate (PEM, optional) field.
